FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is SPLATTR!?

SPLATTR! is a VR paintball game built around hand based movement. It is an independent game that is currently in development.

Is it out yet?

Not yet. Right now there is a forest map and a subway map, with many more on the way. Follow the Discord for news.

How do I move around?

With your hands. The movement system is hand based, so you make your own routes through each map.

How many lives do I get?

Three. Dodge shots, stay alive, and make every life count.

Where can I follow development?

Join the community Discord in the Community section below, and check the Updates section on this page.

Who makes SPLATTR!?

It is made independently. You can find the developer in the Developers section above.
