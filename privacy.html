Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit the SPLATTR! website or use the SPLATTR! game. We aim to collect only what is needed to operate and improve the experience.

1. Who operates SPLATTR!

SPLATTR! is an independent game project operated under the name Funky Monke Studios (“we”, “us”, or “our”). For privacy questions, use the community contact channel linked on the SPLATTR! website.

2. Information handled by the website

The website does not currently provide user accounts, forms, or direct purchases. Like most websites, the hosting provider (Vercel) may process basic request and security information such as IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic logs to deliver and protect the site.

3. Information handled by the game

If you use SPLATTR!, the game may process information needed for multiplayer, account, moderation, and gameplay features. This can include platform or player identifiers, display name, room or matchmaking information, inventory or cosmetic data, gameplay events, and technical diagnostics. Services such as Photon and PlayFab may process information on our behalf to provide networking and game services. Their own privacy policies also apply.

4. Platform and third-party services

SPLATTR! may be accessed through Meta platforms and may link to Discord or other third-party services. Those services are operated independently and have their own privacy policies and controls. We do not control how third parties collect or use information.

5. How information is used

  • To operate multiplayer, accounts, and game features.
  • To maintain game security, prevent abuse, and respond to reports.
  • To diagnose bugs, improve performance, and maintain the website.
  • To communicate important service or development updates where applicable.

6. Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website or game, including hosting, networking, account, and diagnostics providers if enabled. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or our rights.

7. Children and families

SPLATTR! is not intended to ask children to submit personal information through this website. Do not post private information in public usernames, chat, or community spaces. Parents or guardians with a privacy concern may contact us through the community channel. Applicable platform age requirements still apply.

8. Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above or as required by service providers, law, or platform rules. We use the protections available through our providers, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of certain personal information. Requests can be made through the community contact channel. We may need enough information to identify the relevant account, and some records may need to be retained for security or legal reasons.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the website or game changes. The “last updated” date at the top indicates the latest revision.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Funky Monke Studios through the SPLATTR! Discord community.
